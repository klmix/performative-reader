The Performative Reader

Import your Goodreads library, sort your books in a tier list, and redistribute your star ratings along a curve. Supports half and quarter stars, including ratings above 5.

⤓ Drop your Goodreads export here or click to choose goodreads_library_export.csv
  1. Export. On Goodreads open My Books → Import and export and click Export Library. StoryGraph exports work too.
  2. Rank. Your read books are sorted into tiers by their stars. Drag to reorder; further left means better.
  3. Re-rate. Pick a distribution, set limits per rating, apply it, and export in the same format you imported.

Your data is stored in your browser only. Covers come from Open Library.
